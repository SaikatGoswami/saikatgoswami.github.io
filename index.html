About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at TCG CREST, Kolkata. I completed my PhD in Mathematics from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute (RKMVERI) in November 2025.

My research is primarily in non-associative algebra, with particular interests in Lie-Yamaguti algebras, operads, quandles, and related areas of algebra and topology.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Non-associative Algebra
  • Lie-Yamaguti Algebras
  • Algebraic Operads
  • Quandles and Rack Theory
  • Homological and Algebraic Topology
